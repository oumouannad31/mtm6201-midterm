Frequently Asked Questions

What is Bella Onojie?

Bella Onojie is a food ordering app designed to make discovering and ordering your favourite meals simple and convenient.

How do I create an account?

Open the app, select the sign-up option and enter your email address and password to create your account.

How can I place an order?

Browse the available meals, choose your favourites, add them to your cart and proceed to checkout.

What payment methods are accepted?

Available payment methods will be displayed during checkout. You can select your preferred option before confirming your order.

Can I track my order?

Check the order status through the app whenever tracking information is available.

What if I have a problem with my order?

If you experience any issues, visit our Contact page and send our team a message. We will be happy to assist you.
